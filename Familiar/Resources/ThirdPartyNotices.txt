54ca185b77f170e12fd353fcd7443232f6cb73fd.
https://github.com/OpenMinis/ish-arm64

iSH is licensed under GNU GPL version 3. The iSH iOS additional terms and the
corresponding-source offer are bundled as ISH_LICENSE_IOS.txt and
ISHSourceOffer.txt. Familiar's complete GPLv3 text is bundled as GPL-3.0.txt.

Familiar's Linux userspace is assembled from Alpine Linux 3.24.0 aarch64.
https://alpinelinux.org/

Alpine is an aggregation of independently licensed packages. The exact base
image digest and requested package set are recorded in
Vendor/ISHRuntime/manifest.json. Scripts/prepare-ish-rootfs.sh emits the exact
installed package versions and declared licenses beside the prepared rootfs;
that generated inventory must be included in release compliance artifacts.
