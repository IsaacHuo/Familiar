Familiar iSH Corresponding Source

Familiar incorporates source from the iSH ARM64 fork at:
https://github.com/OpenMinis/ish-arm64/tree/54ca185b77f170e12fd353fcd7443232f6cb73fd

The exact upstream source revision is included as the Vendor/ish-arm64 Git
submodule. Familiar's integration source and reproducible build scripts are
available at:
https://github.com/IsaacHuo/Familiar

The iSH source is licensed under GNU GPL version 3, together with the
additional iOS terms reproduced in ISH_LICENSE_IOS.txt. Familiar is distributed
under GNU GPL version 3; the complete license text is reproduced in the root
LICENSE file.

The Alpine root filesystem is assembled from Alpine Linux 3.24.0 aarch64.
Its locked source URL, digest, selected packages, and generated package-license
inventory are defined by Vendor/ISHRuntime/manifest.json and
Scripts/prepare-ish-rootfs.sh.

For a source copy on durable physical media, contact the distributor identified
on Familiar's App Store listing. This offer is valid for at least three years
after the last distribution of the corresponding object code. The charge, if
any, will not exceed the reasonable cost of physically providing the source.
